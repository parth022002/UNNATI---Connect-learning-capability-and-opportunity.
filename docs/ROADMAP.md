# Development roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
- Repository, linting, tests, CI, environment configuration
- Brand tokens, responsive public website, API health endpoint
- Database migrations and authentication integration

## Phase 1 — Learner profile and document ingestion
- Profile and education history
- Secure document upload
- PDF/DOCX parsing, OCR and structured extraction
- Extraction review and correction interface

## Phase 2 — Skills and career knowledge
- Canonical skills taxonomy and aliases
- Curriculum/course-to-skill mapping
- Career role and sector taxonomy
- Role-to-skill requirements with source provenance
- Prerequisite graph

## Phase 3 — Gap analysis and roadmap
- Target role selection
- Evidence-weighted skill gap calculation
- Prerequisite-aware roadmap generation
- Learning resources and projects
- Progress tracking

## Phase 4 — Assessments and AI assistant
- Skill assessments and evidence capture
- RAG career assistant with citations
- Interview and job preparation
- Quality evaluation and feedback loop

## Phase 5 — Institution and employer ecosystem
- Institution dashboards and cohort analytics
- Employer talent discovery with transparent filters
- Opportunity integrations and market intelligence
- Governance, audit and advanced analytics
